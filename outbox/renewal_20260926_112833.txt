Subject: Re: Contract Renewal – Response to Proposed 12% Price Increase

Dear Acme Cloud Services,

Thank you for your renewal proposal for a 12-month contract extension.

We acknowledge your request to renew our cloud services agreement with a 12% price increase citing rising infrastructure costs. However, we must respectfully decline this proposal and offer a counter-offer based on our contractual terms and current market conditions.

**Contractual Position:**

Our existing contract (Clause 8.2) stipulates that renewal price increases are capped at 7% unless both parties agree otherwise in writing. Your proposed 12% increase exceeds this contractual cap and cannot be accepted without formal written amendment, which our procurement policy does not permit for increases of this magnitude.

**Service Performance Credits:**

During the performance period November 2025 through September 2026, your service fell below our agreed SLA target of 99.9% monthly uptime (Clause 4.1) in two months:
- May 2026: 99.42% uptime
- July 2026: 99.61% uptime

Per Clause 4.2, Nimbus Retail is entitled to service credits of 5% on the monthly fee for each month below target. This amounts to Rs 20,000 in unclaimed credits (Rs 10,000 per missed month × 2 months). Before renewal discussions proceed, we require these credits be applied to our account.

**Counter-Offer:**

Nimbus Retail proposes renewal on the following terms:

1. **Price Increase:** 7% on the current annual fee of Rs 24,00,000, in line with Clause 8.2 of our existing contract. This results in a new annual fee of Rs 25,68,000 (Rs 2,14,000 monthly).

2. **Service Credits:** Application of Rs 20,000 in earned SLA credits against the first billing cycle of the renewed term.

3. **Renewal Period:** 12 months, as requested.

Additionally, we have received competitive quotes for equivalent service at 3% to 4% below your current pricing. We value our partnership with Acme Cloud Services, but we must ensure our renewal terms remain commercially competitive and compliant with our contractual framework.

Please confirm your acceptance of this counter-offer to allow adequate time for contract execution before the current term expires on 31 October 2026.

We look forward to your response.

Best regards,

**Nimbus Retail Procurement**
