NIMBUS RETAIL - PROCUREMENT POLICY

Rule 1: Renewal price increases up to 5% may be approved by the Procurement Manager.
Rule 2: Increases above 5% and up to 10% need Finance Director approval and written justification from the vendor.
Rule 3: Increases above 10% must not be accepted. They must be negotiated down or sent to competitive bidding.
Rule 4: If a vendor missed its SLA in the last 12 months, unclaimed service credits must be claimed before renewal.
Rule 5: Every counter-offer to a vendor must reference the relevant contract clause numbers.
